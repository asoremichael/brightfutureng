HOW TO GET STARTED

Your Admission Journey

We've made the process simple and straightforward to help your family get started.

02

03

Complete Application

Obtain and complete the admission application with the required student information.

04

Our admissions team will review the application and communicate the next steps.

WHAT YOU NEED TO APPLY

Admission Requirements

To help us process your child's application, parents and guardians may be asked to provide the following documents:

01

Completed admission application form

02

Recent passport photographs of the student

03

Birth certificate or proof of age

04

Previous school report or academic records, where applicable

05

Medical information and emergency contact details

Additional requirements may apply depending on the student's age and class level. Please contact our admissions team for confirmation.
